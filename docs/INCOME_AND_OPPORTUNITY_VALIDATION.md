## Monthly potential scenarios (not forecasts)

| Scenario | Paid hours/month | Assumed effective net rate | Illustrative monthly net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No accepted work | 0 | — | USD 0 |
| First small payout | 5–15 | USD 5–10/h | USD 25–150 |
| Early repeatability | 20–40 | USD 5–15/h | USD 100–600 |
| Validated part-time flow | 40–80 | USD 10–20/h | USD 400–1,600 |
| High-skill, steady task supply | 80–120 | USD 20–30/h | USD 1,600–3,600 |

These are arithmetic scenarios only. They do not represent platform availability, eligibility, acceptance probability, or expected income. The rate is an assumed *effective net rate*, not an advertised rate. Calculate it using actual net paid amounts divided by all time spent on screening, onboarding, task work, revisions, and administration. Deduct platform fees, FX, taxes, and withdrawal costs separately. Do not multiply advertised hourly rates by assumed hours unless those hours were actually paid.

Bounty work is irregular and should be modeled by reward accepted and paid, not as a stable hourly wage. A single large bounty must not be annualized as recurring monthly income. Do not count internal transfers or approved-but-unpaid rewards as income received.

## Source eligibility contract

Do not label a source fully eligible until evidence confirms:

1. Argentina is supported for the specific program/listing.
2. Experience requirements are compatible with the owner's profile.
3. Interview, video, identity, and assessment requirements are known.
4. Work type matches software, IT, cybersecurity, data, or AI development.
5. Payout method is usable from Argentina, or an explicitly permitted alternative is confirmed in writing.
6. Fees, minimum withdrawal, currency conversion, and timing are documented.
7. The actual application/task flow has been tested where possible.

Use explicit states: `UNKNOWN`, `CONDITIONAL`, `INELIGIBLE`, and `VERIFIED`. Missing values are UNKNOWN, never "not required".

### Stripe-dependent bounties: permitted alternative-payment workflow

Stripe is not an automatic reason to discard a bounty. For Opire, Algora, BountyHub, or another platform:

1. Check the native payout options and the exact platform/bounty terms.
2. If the normal Stripe flow is not workable, prepare a private message to the bounty owner/sponsor proposing PayPal, bank transfer, or another mutually supported method.
3. Obtain written confirmation of amount, currency, fees, timing, acceptance criteria, and payment method before starting work.
4. Verify that the platform rules allow the arrangement. Do not move payment off-platform where prohibited.
5. If not confirmed, set `PAYMENT_UNRESOLVED`; do not treat the bounty as payable or spend substantial time on it.

Opire's official documentation says the bounty creator reviews the PR and arranges payment directly; claiming a bounty does not trigger an automatic payment. This does not prove that the same arrangement is allowed on other platforms. [Opire payment rules](https://docs.opire.dev/rewards/pricing)

## Metrics required for a real forecast

Track monthly by source:
- Applications started, completed, accepted, and rejected.
- Screening, onboarding, unpaid assessment, task, revision, and administration hours.
- Tasks submitted, accepted, rejected, and revised.
- Gross approved, fees, net paid, net withdrawn.
- Total paid and unpaid hours.
- Effective net hourly rate.
- Time to first payout.
- Country, identity, and payment failures.
- Source availability and repeat-task rate.

Use at least 8–12 weeks of real source-level outcomes before presenting a statistical monthly forecast. Until then, display **USD 0 verified** and keep scenarios visibly separate.

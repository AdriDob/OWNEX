## Source eligibility contract

Do not label a source fully eligible until evidence confirms:

1. Argentina is supported for the specific program/listing.
2. Experience requirements are compatible.
3. Interview, video, identity, and assessment requirements are known.
4. Work type matches allowed categories.
5. Payout method is usable from Argentina.
6. Fees, minimum withdrawal, currency conversion, and timing are documented.
7. A real application/task flow has been tested where possible.

Use explicit states: `UNKNOWN`, `CONDITIONAL`, `INELIGIBLE`, and `VERIFIED`. Marketing copy and advertised hourly rates are not proof of access.

## Metrics needed for a real forecast

Track monthly by source: applications started/completed/accepted; screening and onboarding hours; tasks submitted/accepted/rejected/revised; gross approved, fees, net paid, net withdrawn; total paid and unpaid hours; effective net hourly rate; time to first payout; and country/payment failures.

Use at least 8–12 weeks of real source-level results before presenting a meaningful monthly forecast. Until then, show **USD 0 verified** and keep scenarios visibly separate.

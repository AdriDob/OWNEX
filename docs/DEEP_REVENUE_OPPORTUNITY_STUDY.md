# OWNEX — Deep Revenue Opportunity Study
**Research baseline:** 2026-11-09  
**Scope:** OWNEX source catalog, Direct Work Engine, Work Bank, security/bounty workflows, AI coding platforms, remote employment, development repositories, investment/trading modules, and potential OWNEX product monetization.

> **Executive conclusion:** USD 10,000/month is a valid long-term business target to investigate, but it is not supported as a current forecast. The previous `.ai/FINANCIAL_ROADMAP_100K_10K.md` and `.ai/DEV_BOUNTY_MAXIMIZATION_EXTENDED.md` contain arithmetic built on invented success percentages, assumed bounty values, and throughput (for example 40–80 accepted PRs/month) that has not been demonstrated. Their revenue timelines must be treated as aspirational planning notes, not forecasts or verified capability.

- Application assistant and source adapters including Opire, IssueHunt, Freelancer, Open Collective, Outlier, Mindrift, Algora, HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti, YesWeHack and others.

These are capability foundations. They do not establish that external platforms currently expose paid tasks, that a user's account is eligible, that a submission will be accepted, or that a withdrawal will arrive.

## 2. Audit of the old USD 10K–100K projections

The old roadmap assumes, among other things:
- 10–30 paid bug-bounty findings every month.
- 40–80 pull requests per month with 50–75% acceptance and USD 150–500 per accepted PR.
- 80–160 paid freelance hours every month.
- Multiple AI/data projects per month at USD 500–2,000 each.
- A 71.6% to 89.2% "success" improvement that is not a measured historical payout conversion rate.

None of those throughput or acceptance assumptions is supported by OWNEX's source-level paid-outcomes history. The source adaptation snapshot also shows zero Argentina-verified and zero production-active sources. Therefore, the old monthly totals of USD 12K, USD 33.2K, USD 79K and the 2–6 month claims are **withdrawn as forecasts**. They may remain as hypothetical ambition examples only if clearly labelled as unvalidated.

A test suite can verify that an engine scores and moves an opportunity through a workflow. It cannot prove that a platform has paid that opportunity.

## 4. What USD 10,000/month mathematically requires

USD 10K/month can arise from several different economic structures. These are unit-economics examples, not claims that OWNEX or its owner currently has these customers, hours, or conversion rates.

### A. Employment + variable technical work
- One remote software role at USD 3,000/month gross.
- 80 genuinely paid coding-evaluation hours at USD 30/hour = USD 2,400 gross.
- USD 4,600/month still needs another validated stream.

This is a possible diversified stack only if the hours are actually offered and compatible with the employment contract. Outlier's official FAQ describes contractors, selective screening, qualification, and weekly PayPal/AirTM/ACH payments; its coding pages advertise rates that vary by project and expertise. Do not assume 160 billable hours or continuous task availability.

### B. Productized software / automation
- 10 customers × USD 1,000/month = USD 10,000 MRR.
- 20 customers × USD 500/month = USD 10,000 MRR.
- 50 customers × USD 200/month = USD 10,000 MRR.

This is recurring revenue before hosting, support, acquisition, refunds, taxes, payment fees and churn. It requires a repeatable outcome, not custom one-off development for every customer.

### C. OWNEX as a SaaS product
- 100 customers × USD 99/month = USD 9,900 MRR.
- 50 customers × USD 199/month = USD 9,950 MRR.
- 25 teams × USD 399/month = USD 9,975 MRR.

The existing personal desktop application cannot simply be relabelled SaaS. A commercial version would need tenant isolation, secure credential storage, data boundaries, billing, onboarding, support, usage limits, provider terms, privacy/security review and a distribution channel. A paid desktop license or self-hosted business tier may be a better first test than multi-tenant cloud hosting.

### D. Bug bounty / security research
A USD 10K month could be produced by one exceptional critical payout or several accepted reports. It should not be forecast as a fixed monthly salary. Immunefi reported USD 4.6M in researcher payouts in Q3 2026 and USD 1.46M in September, with 278 researchers paid that month; this proves that large payouts exist in the ecosystem, not that a new researcher has a predictable share. Payout distribution is highly unequal and timing is controlled by program triage.

### E. Trading returns and capital requirement
At a purely arithmetic level, USD 10K/month would require:
- USD 1,000,000 of capital at 1% net monthly return.
- USD 500,000 at 2% net monthly return.
- USD 200,000 at 5% net monthly return.

These are not safe or dependable return assumptions. Sustaining 2–5% net monthly is extremely difficult; losses, drawdowns, fees, slippage, taxes and changing market regimes can erase capital. Trading should not be included in the baseline income forecast, and live execution must remain disabled until a long, independently checked paper-trading record supports it.

## 6. Recommended OWNEX source scoring: stop counting names

Every source and every opportunity should carry separate evidence fields:

1. **Access:** Argentina supported for this exact program; account registration works.
2. **Work eligibility:** experience, degree, portfolio, English, interview, video, test, equipment, and legal requirements.
3. **Inventory:** live open tasks/jobs, freshness, funded amount, and task availability.
4. **Execution:** API/browser/manual access, login support, rate limits, terms of service, and whether the task can be prepared safely.
5. **Economics:** reward/salary, acceptance probability from OWNEX history only, human hours, fees, FX, tax, and effective net hourly rate.
6. **Payout:** payment rail, Argentina withdrawal path, minimum, fees, timing, and successful proof.
7. **Repeatability:** repeat task rate, buyer/maintainer response, recurring demand, and concentration risk.

Use distinct statuses:
- `CATALOG_ONLY`: name/URL only.
- `DISCOVERABLE`: public listings can be found.
- `ADAPTER_READY`: OWNEX can ingest or prepare a task.
- `ACCOUNT_ACCESS_VERIFIED`: user can log in and access the relevant product.
- `ARGENTINA_PAYOUT_VERIFIED`: documented and tested withdrawal route.
- `LIVE_TASK_VERIFIED`: an actual eligible funded task or job is open.
- `PAID_ONCE`: first real payout evidenced.
- `REPEATABLE`: at least 3 paid outcomes over 8–12 weeks.
- `PRODUCTION_ACTIVE`: monitored, stable, and safe enough for regular use.

Do not collapse these statuses into one "verified" boolean. A platform can have a working public API but no Argentina payout, or an available payout method but no tasks.

## 7. Revenue measurement: forecast from evidence

Per source and month, record:
- opportunities discovered, eligible, applied, accepted, completed, rejected, and paid;
- time spent searching, screening, assessment, onboarding, delivery, revision and admin;
- gross amount approved, fees, net paid, net withdrawn, currency and FX rate;
- time from application to first task and from accepted task to payout;
- source-specific acceptance rate and effective net USD/hour;
- repeat purchase/retention for products and services;
- confidence interval based on actual history, not manually assigned success percentages.

Forecast tiers:
- **Actual:** paid and externally evidenced.
- **P50/P90 forecast:** only after sufficient source-level history; include uncertainty and sample size.
- **Scenario:** arithmetic "what if" calculation, never displayed as expected income.
- **Advertised rate:** provider marketing data, never treated as realized or guaranteed.

A meaningful OWNEX source forecast requires at least 8–12 weeks of observations and enough completed outcomes per source. Until then, verified revenue remains USD 0.

## 8. Priority plan to pursue the USD 10K/month goal

### P0 — First 30 days: build the base and validate fast paths
1. Apply to suitable remote junior roles, starting with MixRank and fresh Argentina-eligible listings; verify every job at the employer page.
2. Finish one practical Python/FastAPI/PostgreSQL portfolio project with tests, README, short demo and clear ownership. Use OWNEX/Rastro only where the code can be demonstrated reliably.
3. Complete one AI coding-platform assessment only if its current project requirements fit the user's education/skill profile and payout route. Do not spend weeks on generic waitlists.
4. Validate the 17 adapter-ready sources one by one; start with sources already mapped to real adapters, not new catalog additions.
5. Run a small, strictly in-scope security research workflow on selected programs; measure hours and report quality. No automated mass scanning.
6. Separate work that requires client sales from employment and bounty work; do not let freelance proposals silently become the default.

### P1 — Days 30–90: specialize and prove conversion
1. Pick one main technical revenue lane: Python/backend automation or API/security automation. Do not attempt game dev, AI, quant, web3, data engineering and QA simultaneously.
2. Build a reusable deliverable from that lane: e.g. API integration monitor, data extraction/validation tool, security evidence/reporting workflow, or a focused developer utility.
3. Seek repeatable paid tasks and funded bounties, tracking actual paid hourly value.
4. Validate one productized B2B offer or one paid developer tool with real buyer conversations/pre-orders before building a large product.
5. Run weather/Polymarket/crypto strategies in paper mode only; log enough trades to measure out-of-sample performance.

### P2 — Months 3–12: build recurring revenue
1. Productize the most repeated paid problem as a small software product, subscription, license, or business tier.
2. Test pricing and distribution with a narrow customer group before adding multi-tenant SaaS complexity.
3. Keep one stable income base while recurring revenue is still small.
4. Add contractors/automation only after customer demand and margins are proven.
5. Treat USD 10K/month as a milestone reached only after 3 consecutive months of collected net revenue at or above the target, not one exceptional bounty or one month of gross billings.

## 9. Final verdict

- **USD 10K/month is possible in principle** through a combination of high-value employment/contracting, repeatable software products, specialized security work, or a mix of these.
- **It is not currently demonstrated by OWNEX.** The repository's own latest source snapshot shows 0 integration-verified, 0 Argentina-verified and 0 production-active sources.
- The strongest scalable path is not to collect 500 platforms. It is to convert a small number of high-value channels into repeatable paid outcomes, then productize what repeats.
- For the user's current skill and stability needs, the recommended sequence is: stable junior role or paid technical base → specialization and evidence → repeatable paid deliverable → product/subscription scale. Bug bounty and trading remain upside paths, not the household budget.
- Do not merge projections that claim USD 10K/month in 2–6 months as if they were researched forecasts. This report supersedes those unsupported numbers until real conversion and payout data exists.
